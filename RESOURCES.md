# Learning Resources

A curated index of sources used to ground lessons and knowledge notes.

## Source selection
Prefer, when suitable:
1. Primary sources: original research, official statistics, legislation, standards, documentation, original speeches, archival records, and first-hand datasets.
2. Authoritative secondary sources: academic textbooks, peer-reviewed reviews, reputable universities, professional institutions, and high-quality reference works.
3. High-quality explanations: established journalism, expert lectures, specialist books, and educational material.

Evaluate sources in context. Primary sources can still be biased, incomplete, or difficult to interpret. Record disagreements and uncertainty.

## Resource record
For important recurring sources, record title, author/organization, domain, source type (primary/secondary/explanatory), URL, why useful, last checked date, and limitations.
