# Domains

Domains provide broad navigation and a home for field-level maps. They are not exclusive ownership boundaries: a concept can be linked and tagged across domains while retaining one canonical note.

Create domains/<domain>/README.md when a field has enough material to benefit from a landing page. Include what the domain covers, core questions and subfields, important topic syntheses, key atomic notes, and connections to other domains.

Do not create empty domain folders just to fill out a taxonomy.
