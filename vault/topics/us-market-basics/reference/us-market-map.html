US Market Basics

Keep the market, asset, fund, index, account and option separate.

Market structure

TermMeaning
Primary marketNew securities are issued; the issuer receives the proceeds.
Secondary marketInvestors trade existing securities with one another.
Stock exchangeA regulated trading venue, such as Nasdaq or NYSE.

Investment objects

TermMeaning
StockA share of ownership in one company.
FundA pooled portfolio that invests in assets such as stocks or bonds.
ETFA fund whose shares trade on an exchange during the trading day.
IndexA measurement of a selected group of securities; you cannot buy the index directly.
Index fundA mutual fund or ETF designed to follow an index.

Four widely followed US indexes

IndexWhat it follows
Nasdaq CompositeThousands of stocks listed on Nasdaq.
Nasdaq-100About 100 of the largest non-financial Nasdaq-listed companies.
S&P 500500 leading large US companies; about 80% of available US market capitalisation.
Dow Jones Industrial Average30 large US companies; price-weighted.

Nasdaq is both an exchange name and part of several index names. The Dow is not the S&P 500.

Retirement

PlanBasic structure
401(k)Defined-contribution workplace plan. Contributions go into an account; investments determine much of the final balance.
Traditional pensionDefined-benefit plan. Retirement income is promised through a formula, often based on salary and service.
Social SecurityPublic social-insurance programme funded mainly by payroll taxes, not a personal stock-market account.

Remember

A retirement account is the container. A fund is an investment inside it. The index is the benchmark the fund may follow.

Options

TermBuyer has the right to…
CallBuy the underlying asset at the strike price.
PutSell the underlying asset at the strike price.
StrikeThe fixed price written in the contract.
ExpirationThe date when the option ends.
PremiumThe price paid by the option buyer.

A standard US stock option generally represents 100 shares. Buyers can lose the full premium. Some option sellers can lose much more.
