# Mission: Understand the US Stock Market

## Why
Read US financial news and online trading discussions without confusing the market, the asset, the benchmark, the account and the contract. Understand how ordinary investors, funds, options and retirement plans connect, so I can make informed decisions and recognise risk.

## Success looks like
- Explain the difference between primary and secondary markets and why liquidity matters.
- Distinguish a stock, a fund, an ETF, an index and an index fund.
- Explain the difference between Nasdaq the exchange, the Nasdaq Composite, the Nasdaq-100, the S&P 500 and the Dow.
- Explain how a 401(k), a traditional pension and Social Security differ.
- Read a basic call or put option description and identify the main risks.

## Constraints
- Use clear B1-level English, with Simplified Chinese glosses for technical terms on first use.
- Prefer layered explanations, concrete examples and high-trust primary sources.
- Teach market structure before advanced trading strategies.

## Out of scope
- Personal investment, tax or legal advice.
- Advanced options strategies, option pricing models and portfolio construction.
