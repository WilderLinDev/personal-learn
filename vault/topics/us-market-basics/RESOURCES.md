# US Market Basics Resources

## Knowledge

- [SEC Investor.gov: Stocks — FAQs](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks)
  The SEC's plain-language explanation of what a share represents and why companies issue stock. Use for stock ownership and shareholder rights.
- [SEC Investor.gov: Secondary Market](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/secondary-market)
  Defines the secondary market as trading in existing securities. Use to distinguish investor-to-investor trading from new issuance.
- [SEC Investor.gov: Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/mutual-funds-and-exchange-traded-2)
  Explains ETF portfolios, trading, diversification, fees and risks. Use for ETF and fund structure.
- [SEC Investor.gov: Index Funds](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/mutual-funds-and-exchange-traded-4)
  Explains that an index fund can be a mutual fund or ETF, and that an index itself cannot be bought directly. Use for the index/fund distinction.
- [SEC Investor.gov: An Introduction to Options](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-63)
  SEC options bulletin, updated 16 July 2026. Covers calls, puts, strike prices, premiums, contract size, examples and risks. Use as the primary options reference.
- [IRS: Retirement Plans Definitions](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-plans-definitions)
  Official definitions of 401(k), defined-contribution plans, defined-benefit plans and IRAs. Use to distinguish the account or plan from the investments held inside it.
- [U.S. Department of Labor: Types of Retirement Plans](https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/retirement/typesofplans)
  Explains defined-benefit and defined-contribution plans, including 401(k)s. Use for the difference between a traditional pension promise and an individual account.
- [Social Security Administration: Fast Facts & Figures About Social Security, 2026](https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/chartbooks/fast_facts/2026/fast_facts26.html)
  Explains Social Security financing. Use to distinguish the public social-insurance programme from a personal investment account.
- [Nasdaq: The Nasdaq Stock Market](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/north-american-markets/nasdaq-stock-market)
  Describes Nasdaq as an exchange and trading venue. Use when separating the exchange from Nasdaq-branded indexes.
- [Nasdaq: Nasdaq Composite](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/global-indexes/nasdaq-composite)
  Explains the broad index of stocks listed on the Nasdaq Stock Market.
- [Nasdaq: Nasdaq Composite vs. Nasdaq-100](https://www.nasdaq.com/newsroom/nasdaq-composite-vs-nasdaq-100-what-investors-should-know)
  Explains why the two Nasdaq indexes are different. Use when a market screen says “Nasdaq”.
- [S&P Dow Jones Indices: S&P 500](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/)
  Official index description: 500 leading companies and about 80% of available US market capitalisation.
- [S&P Dow Jones Indices: Dow Jones Industrial Average](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/dow-jones-industrial-average/)
  Official description of the Dow as a price-weighted index of 30 US blue-chip companies.
