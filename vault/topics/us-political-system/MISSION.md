# Mission: Understand the US Political System

## Why
Build a working model of how political power is organised in the United States. Use it to follow US news, identify which institution or level of government has authority, and understand why political decisions can be slow or contested.

## Success looks like
- Explain the three federal branches and give a real example of how they check one another.
- Distinguish federal, state and local responsibilities in a practical news story.
- Explain how the two major parties compete for power without confusing parties with constitutional institutions.
- Interpret public-opinion numbers with attention to dates, populations and differences within each party.

## Constraints
- Use clear B1-level English, with Simplified Chinese glosses for technical terms on first use.
- Prefer layered explanations, mechanisms and concrete examples.
- Use current, high-trust sources and date public-opinion data.

## Out of scope
- Detailed history of every political party or Supreme Court case.
- Predicting election outcomes or recommending political choices.
- Detailed legal advice about a particular state's laws.
