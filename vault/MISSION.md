# Mission: A broad, connected understanding of the world

## Why
Build a broad, connected understanding of the world while improving reasoning, judgment, and practical skills — to make better decisions in unfamiliar situations. This is a long-term, multi-disciplinary mission, not a single course or credential.

## Success looks like
- Explain how systems, people, institutions, and the natural world work, using mental models rather than disconnected facts — across economics, finance, history, science, technology, psychology, philosophy, politics, and other fields.
- Explain causes and mechanisms; evaluate evidence, sources, incentives, and uncertainty; compare explanations; recognise assumptions and second-order effects.
- Transfer ideas between disciplines.
- Apply knowledge in real situations through projects, exercises, decisions, and feedback.
- Read and write clear, natural English about these subjects, with growing complexity over time.

## Constraints
- English is both the learning medium and a learning goal; durable materials are in English, discussion often in Chinese.
- Do not force unrelated English exercises into subject learning.
