# Mission

## North star
Build a broad, connected understanding of the world while improving reasoning, judgment, and practical skills. A long-term, multi-disciplinary mission, not a single course or credential.

## Long-term goals
1. **Broad knowledge foundation.** Economics, finance, history, science, technology, psychology, philosophy, politics, and other fields. Build mental models of how systems, people, institutions, and the natural world work, rather than memorising disconnected facts.
2. **Reasoning and judgment.** Explain causes and mechanisms; evaluate evidence, sources, incentives, and uncertainty; compare explanations; recognise assumptions and second-order effects; transfer ideas between disciplines; decide better in unfamiliar situations.
3. **Practical skills.** Apply knowledge through projects, exercises, decisions, and feedback.
4. **English.** English is both a medium and a goal: durable notes are written in clear, natural English that is challenging but understandable, increasing in complexity over time.

## What good progress looks like
- I can explain important ideas clearly, without notes.
- I can apply them to new situations.
- Misconceptions are corrected and open questions are recorded.
- Knowledge is connected across domains.
