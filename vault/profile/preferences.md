# Preferences

## Language
- Conversation: Mandarin Chinese, or mixed Chinese/English. Chinese is often best for clarifying difficult ideas.
- Written notes: clear, accessible English. Preserve important English terms and explain them in Chinese when helpful.
- Prefer layered explanations over sentence-by-sentence translation.
- Do not force unrelated English exercises into subject learning.

## Teaching
- Teach across many disciplines, not only software engineering.
- Explain mechanisms, assumptions, examples, and limitations.
- Connect new topics to what I already know when the link is meaningful.
- Keep sessions focused; allow deeper follow-up when I ask.
- Do not overuse quizzes beyond what the protocol needs.

## Avoid
- Raw chat transcripts as notes.
- Dependence on a proprietary note-taking app (Obsidian is not required).

## Persistence
- Repository: WilderLinDev/personal-learn
- Auto-commit sessions to the default branch (`main`): yes, without asking each time.
- Visibility of this vault: public, by explicit choice. Detailed progress, weak areas, and reflections may be included; do not redact them.
- Future: a web-based browsing experience is preferred.
