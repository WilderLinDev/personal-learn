# Learning Mission

## North star
Build a broad, connected understanding of the world while improving reasoning, judgment, and practical skills. This is a long-term, multi-disciplinary mission, not a single course or credential.

## Three goals

### 1. Build a broad knowledge foundation
Explore economics, finance, history, science, technology, psychology, philosophy, politics, and other relevant fields. Build mental models of how systems, people, institutions, and the natural world work rather than memorizing disconnected facts.

### 2. Improve reasoning and judgment
Learn to explain causes and mechanisms; evaluate evidence, sources, incentives, and uncertainty; compare explanations; recognize assumptions and second-order effects; transfer ideas between disciplines; and make better decisions in unfamiliar situations.

### 3. Acquire practical skills
Develop skills that can be applied in real situations. Use projects, exercises, decisions, and feedback to test whether knowledge works beyond a lesson.

## English development
English is both a learning medium and a learning goal.
- Write durable knowledge documents in clear English.
- Prefer plain, natural English that is challenging but understandable.
- Preserve important English terms.
- Use Chinese explanations when they improve comprehension.
- Gradually increase English complexity as comprehension improves.
- Prioritize the subject being learned; do not force unrelated language exercises into every lesson.

## What good learning produces
1. A clear explanation of important ideas.
2. A record of what the learner can explain or apply.
3. Corrected misconceptions and unresolved questions.
4. Reusable knowledge notes and meaningful links.
5. A realistic next step and review schedule.

## Mission changes
Do not silently change this mission. When the user explicitly changes long-term goals, update this file and create a learning record explaining the change.
